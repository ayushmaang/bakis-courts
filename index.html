COURT QUESTIONS

Good to know.

A quick overview of the basketball court and the park around it.

What is Baki's Courts?

It is the outdoor basketball court at Belmont Trace Park in Brambleton, Virginia.

Where is the court?

The court pin is on Franklin Benjamin Terrace, Brambleton, VA 20148. Belmont Trace Park sits along Connie Marie Terrace, near Belmont Ridge Road and south of Creighton Road. Check the map pin before traveling.

When is the park open?

Published park guidance lists access from dawn to dusk. This is not a reservation or a guarantee that the basketball court will be available.

Who may visit?

Published park guidance limits access to Brambleton owners, residents, and their guests. This independent guide does not grant access or change park rules.

Who do I contact?

For court operations at Baki's Courts, call +1 (571) 295-2452. Park access rules and maintenance may be handled separately by park management.
